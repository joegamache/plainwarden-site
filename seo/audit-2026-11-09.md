## Technical verification

- Live crawl: all 24 URLs in the original production sitemap returned HTTP 200. Robots and sitemap returned 200; the robots file allows public content and excludes portal/billing/checkout paths.
- HTTP/apex requests resolved to `https://www.plainwarden.com/`. A deliberately nonexistent page returned HTTP 404, with no soft-404 homepage fallback.
- Sample PDF and social image returned the expected content types. Representative clean page URLs returned HTTP 200 before changes.
- HTTPS, HSTS and gzip compression were present. No public-page `X-Robots-Tag` indexing block was seen in the sampled responses.
- The four deliberately nonindexable HTML pages—Terms, Privacy, Refunds and Thanks—remain excluded from the sitemap and marked noindex. Terms/Privacy/Refunds legal source text was not changed.
- Build succeeded. The added standard-library validator passed for all 28 HTML pages, 24 sitemap targets, canonical/OG URL agreement, one primary H1, unique description/OG title tags, parseable JSON-LD, article date ordering, local file links and anchors, noindex exclusions and direct article/guide CTAs.
- Browser QA at 390 × 844: homepage, FTC article and sample page had document width 390, with no horizontal document overflow. Homepage was visually inspected; article and sample content/links were inspected in the browser.
- The live Start your plan link opened the intended Tally questionnaire, including the expected plan parameter. A raw HTTP fetch to Tally returned 403, but browser access succeeded. No answers were submitted; payment, order delivery, plan generation, subscription management and refunds were not exercised.
- These checks establish technical readiness, not confirmed search indexing, rankings, conversion lift or full legal compliance.

## Search intent and conversion assessment

The existing structure covers distinct intents without needing more near-duplicate pages:

| Visitor intent | Best existing page | Recommended success signal |
|---|---|---|
| Buy/create a WISP for a tax practice | Homepage | Qualified questionnaire starts and paid orders |
| Inspect a WISP example before buying | Sample plan | Sample-to-questionnaire progression |
| Understand whether a WISP is required | Do tax preparers need a WISP? | Relevant impressions, engaged reading and qualified starts |
| Understand the FTC requirements and small-firm exception | FTC Safeguards article | Search visibility and progression to sample/checklist |
| Find a checklist or DIY starting point | WISP checklist / Publication 5708 article | Useful reading, guide visits and optional product starts |
| Understand the PTIN renewal question | PTIN renewal article | Seasonal query performance and assisted orders |
| Implement a safeguard | Individual how-to guides | Useful completion and return visits; optional WISP starts |

The commercial page should continue to sell an editable, practice-specific plan and action list. Educational articles should answer the question first, with a clear optional next step. Preserve the free IRS source links and the distinction between preparing a document and operating a security program. Prices, checkout destinations, Tally form and legal policies were preserved.

## Regulatory and search references checked

- [16 CFR 314.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-314.6): exceptions apply to §314.4(b)(1), (d)(2), (h), (i), based on maintained customer information concerning fewer than 5,000 consumers.
- [16 CFR 314.4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-314.4): risk assessment/reassessment, encryption/MFA alternatives, regular testing/monitoring and the prescribed information-system testing regime.
- [FTC Safeguards guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftc-safeguards-rule-what-your-business-needs-know): coverage and notification events involving unauthorized acquisition of at least 500 consumers' unencrypted information, as soon as possible and within 30 days of discovery.
- [IRS Publication 5708](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5708.pdf): sample information for a practice-specific plan, not a substitute for implementing safeguards or assessing the firm's needs.
- [IRS Section 7216 information center](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/section-7216-information-center): consent rules and permitted regulatory exceptions.
- [Google FAQ rich-result guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes): FAQ markup is retained because it reflects visible content; a commercial WISP site should not expect FAQ rich results reserved for authoritative government/health sites.
