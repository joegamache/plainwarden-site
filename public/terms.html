PlainWarden is documentation software for tax, accounting and similar firms. You answer a questionnaire about your practice, and the software assembles a Written Information Security Plan ("Plan") from pre-written content. The Plan records the practices you report, identifies potential gaps and items to confirm, and provides generally applicable compliance-support information with regulatory references. Depending on what you buy, the Service also includes the subscription features listed in section 6 (together, the "Service").
