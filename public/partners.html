How it works

  1. Apply with the short form. We reply within 2 business days.
  2. If approved, you sign a one-page partner agreement and get your personal link.
  3. Anyone who arrives through your link and buys within 60 days counts as your referral.
  4. Each month we email you what you earned and pay it.

Who it suits
