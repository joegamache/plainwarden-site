3. How we use it

To generate and deliver your Plan; provide the subscription features you buy; process payments and renewals; keep records of your consents; send required notices such as renewal reminders; provide support; improve the Service; prevent fraud; and meet legal obligations. With your consent, we may send occasional product news, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

5. Cookies and advertising

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Before we add any, we will update this policy to describe them and any choices you have.

6. How long we keep it

Questionnaire answers and Plan files: while you are a customer, then deleted within 12 months after your Plan is delivered (Plan only) or after your subscription ends, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or a legal hold applies. Records of purchases and consents: at least 3 years, or 1 year after your subscription ends if that is later. Payment records: 7 years for tax purposes. Support emails: 3 years. Deletion covers the providers listed above, on their own deletion schedules.

7. How we protect it

We use two-step login on the accounts that hold your information, rely on our providers' encryption in transit and at rest, and limit access to the people who need it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or opt out of marketing, by emailing support@plainwarden.com. We'll respond within 30 days. California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18.
