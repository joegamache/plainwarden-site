5. Cookies, advertising and measurement

We advertise PlainWarden with Google Ads. To learn whether our ads lead to purchases, our website uses Google's advertising tag, which sets cookies and similar technologies on pages of plainwarden.com, including our order confirmation page.

When you visit after clicking one of our ads, Google may receive a cookie identifier, your IP address, browser and device information, the pages you view and, for a purchase, the order value and a random order reference. We do not send Google your name, email address, questionnaire answers or Plan. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. We use it only to measure our advertising. We do not use it to build audiences or show you personalized ads.

Your choices: our website honors the Global Privacy Control signal; if your browser sends it, the advertising tag does not load. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com. Our questionnaire, customer portal and emails do not use advertising cookies.

Referral and channel tags: if you reach our website through a partner's link, one of our posts or emails, or an ad, our website stores a short tag in your browser naming that partner or channel (for example a partner code, or "reddit"). Channel tags last until you close the tab. A partner code is kept for 60 days, so the partner who referred you is credited if you come back later. The tag is added to the questionnaire and saved with your order so we can pay partners and see which channels work. It does not identify you, and we do not give partners your name, contact details or answers. You can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.

6. How long we keep it

Questionnaire answers and Plan files: while you are a customer, then deleted within 12 months after your Plan is delivered (Plan only) or after your subscription ends, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or a legal hold applies. Records of purchases and consents: at least 3 years, or 1 year after your subscription ends if that is later. Payment records: 7 years for tax purposes. Support emails: 3 years. Deletion covers the providers listed above, on their own deletion schedules.

7. How we protect it

We use two-step login on the accounts that hold your information, rely on our providers' encryption in transit and at rest, and limit access to the people who need it.

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies, and we won't treat you differently for using them.
