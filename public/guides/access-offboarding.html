Individual logins, access reviews and offboarding

Give each person their own login with only the access they need, review access yearly, and remove it the day someone leaves.

Why it matters

Shared logins make it impossible to tell who did what, and former staff with working passwords are a common source of breaches. The FTC Safeguards Rule requires limiting access to customer information to authorized users who need it.

Steps

  1. Create a separate login for each person in every system, and stop using shared accounts.
  2. Give each person only the access their job needs.
  3. Once a year, list who can access each system and remove anyone who shouldn't.
  4. When someone leaves, on their last day: disable their logins, remove them from the password manager and shared vaults, change any shared passwords, collect devices and keys, and update your IRS e-File application if they were listed.
  5. Keep a short offboarding checklist and note the date each step was done.

You're done when

  • Everyone has their own login.
  • An access review in the past year is recorded.
  • Offboarding is done the same day.

References: 16 CFR 314.4(c)(1); IRS Publication 4557
