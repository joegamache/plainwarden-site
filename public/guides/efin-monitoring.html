Watch your EFIN during filing season

Check each week during filing season how many returns have been filed under your EFIN, so you spot misuse early.

Why it matters

Criminals who steal a preparer's credentials file fake returns under the firm's EFIN. The IRS recommends checking the count of returns filed with your EFIN regularly during filing season.

Steps

  1. Sign in to IRS e-Services and open your e-File application to see your EFIN status and the number of returns filed.
  2. Set a weekly reminder from January through the October extension deadline.
  3. Compare the IRS count with the number your software shows you transmitted.
  4. If the IRS count is higher than yours, contact the IRS e-Help Desk right away and tell your Coordinator.
  5. Keep your e-File application current when staff, addresses or principals change.

You're done when

  • A weekly check is on the calendar for filing season.
  • Checks are noted (date and counts).

References: IRS Publication 4557; IRS Publication 3112
