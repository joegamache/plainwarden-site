Exchange documents securely

Send and receive client documents through a secure portal or encrypted email, never as ordinary email attachments or texts.

Why it matters

Ordinary email can be intercepted, forwarded or sit unprotected in a mailbox for years. The FTC Safeguards Rule requires customer information to be encrypted in transit.

Steps

  1. Pick one or two approved channels: a client portal from your tax software or document provider, or encrypted email (for example, Microsoft 365 message encryption or a secure email service).
  2. Turn on two-step login for the portal and for staff accounts on it.
  3. Tell clients how to send you documents, and include the instructions in your engagement letter.
  4. If a client emails an SSN or tax document, move it into your secure system, delete the email from your mailbox, and remind the client of the secure option.
  5. Never send passwords for encrypted files in the same message as the file. Share them by phone or text.
  6. Confirm unexpected requests to send information or change payment details by calling a number you already have.

You're done when

  • Staff know the approved channels and use them.
  • Your engagement letter or client instructions name the approved channel.

References: 16 CFR 314.4(c)(3); IRS Publication 4557
