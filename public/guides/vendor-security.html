Check a vendor's security

Choose providers that can protect client information, require it in writing, and review them each year.

Why it matters

Your tax software, email, cloud storage and IT provider hold or can reach your client data. The FTC Safeguards Rule requires firms to select capable providers, require safeguards by contract, and periodically assess them.

Steps

  1. Keep a list of every provider that can access client information (the Vendors page in your portal does this).
  2. Before you sign up, ask how they protect data: encryption, two-step login, breach notification and, for larger providers, a security report such as SOC 2.
  3. Check the contract or terms of service for a commitment to protect your information and tell you about a breach. If it's missing, ask for a data protection addendum.
  4. Turn on two-step login on your account with each provider and limit who at your firm has access.
  5. Once a year, confirm each provider still meets these points and you still need them. Record the date.
  6. When you stop using a provider, export what you need and have them delete the rest.

You're done when

  • Every provider on your list has a review date in the past year.
  • Contracts with providers that hold client data include security terms.

References: 16 CFR 314.4(f)
