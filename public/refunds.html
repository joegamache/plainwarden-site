Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied, email support@plainwarden.com within 14 days of any charge, including an annual renewal, and we'll refund that charge in full. No questions asked.

How refunds work

Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days. A full refund of a Year One purchase or renewal ends that subscription and all future billing. A partial refund has only the effect stated in the provision that authorizes it.

After 14 days

Charges are non-refundable after 14 days, except as described above. You can still cancel at any time; cancellation stops future renewal charges as soon as you submit it, and you keep access through the end of the period you paid for.

Cancel anytime

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your portal or any renewal email, or email support@plainwarden.com. No particular subject line is required. We confirm your cancellation by email. You keep every Plan we've already delivered.
