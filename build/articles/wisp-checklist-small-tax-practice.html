Use this checklist to see where your practice stands. Each item links to a short how-to guide.

People and responsibilities

  • A named Data Security Coordinator, with a backup.
  • Every person with access has their own login and signs the rules of behavior. Guide
  • Yearly security training for everyone, and for new hires when they start.
  • Access removed on a person's last day. Guide

Accounts and devices

  • Two-step login on email, tax software, cloud storage, client portal and remote access. Guide
  • Unique passwords in a password manager. Guide
  • Encryption on computers, phones, external drives and backups. Guide
  • Automatic updates, security software and non-administrator accounts for daily work. Guide
  • Router and printer default passwords changed, and a guest Wi-Fi network. Guide

Client data

  • Documents exchanged only through a secure portal or encrypted email. Guide
  • Encrypted backups, with a restore tested in the past year. Guide
  • Retention periods written down, paper locked up, and secure disposal. Guide
  • Client consent on file for any use of tax return information beyond preparing returns. Guide
  • Rules for AI tools, with no client data in free or personal accounts. Guide

Monitoring and vendors

  • Sign-in alerts and activity logs on, with someone reviewing them. Guide
  • Weekly EFIN checks during filing season. Guide
  • A vendor list with security terms in contracts and yearly reviews. Guide

Documents

  • The written plan, signed by the Coordinator and reviewed yearly.
  • Risk assessment and incident response checklist (required at 5,000 or more consumers; recommended for everyone).
  • Staff acknowledgments, device inventory and a list of people with access.
  • A yearly walkthrough of your breach checklist. Guide
